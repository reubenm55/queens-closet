QUEEN'S CLOSET — STARTER BOUTIQUE WEBSITE

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. The brand name is already set to "QUEEN'S CLOSET" in index.html.
2. Replace the sample product names, descriptions, prices and photo links in the products array near the bottom of index.html.
3. Find 2547XXXXXXXX in index.html and replace it with your WhatsApp number in international format, without + or spaces. Example format for Kenya: 2547XXXXXXXX.
4. Test every product, cart quantity button and WhatsApp checkout.
5. The current products and prices are samples. This starter site does not process card or M-Pesa payments, store customer accounts, or have an online admin dashboard.

NOTES
- Product photos are loaded from Unsplash over the internet, so previewing images requires an internet connection.
- This is a front-end starter website. To accept real online payments or manage products securely, you will need a backend/payment integration later.
